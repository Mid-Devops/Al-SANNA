Our Process

Precision execution from concept through site installation.

Step 01

Design

Client consultation, structural load assessment, and 3D conceptual modeling.

Step 02

Detailing

CAD workshop drawings, mold pattern detailing, and resin/reinforcement matrices.

Step 03

Fabrication

Controlled spray-up, filament winding, contact molding, and thermal post-curing.

Step 04

Quality Check

Thickness gauging, Barcol hardness verification, hydrostatic testing, and QA signoff.

Step 05

Installation

On-site delivery, structural erection, certified anchoring, and commissioning support.
